IBRA

Your AI writes the plan.
IBRA runs it.

A Hebrew-first iOS app for people who already train and already use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Available in Israel.

What IBRA does

You answer once: goal, equipment, time, injuries, what you eat. IBRA writes a briefing for your AI. You share it to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini; the AI returns a full training and nutrition plan in a format IBRA reads. Paste it back, or share it straight from the chat, and the plan is live: a daily workout player with the weight from last time, rest timers on the lock screen, meals to tick and weigh, and measurements.

The weekly close

At the end of every week IBRA packs what actually happened (sets and weights, meals, weight and waist, sleep and heart rate if you allow it, photos every three weeks) into one package for your AI coach. The coach returns a list of changes. You approve, and next week starts from the updated plan. Nothing changes without your approval.

What IBRA is not

  • Not an AI coach. Your own AI writes the plan; IBRA runs it and measures.
  • No nagging. No streaks, no points.
  • Your data is never sent to the AI by IBRA. You share, when you choose.
  • No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Data lives on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

A measured rank

Three ranks, Ben Chayil · Ish Chayil · Gibor Chayil, earned on five measured conditions: shoulder-to-waist ratio, body fat, muscle mass relative to height, posture, and a fitness test. The rank is set only at the weekly close; it cannot be chosen.

Price and requirements

First calibration week free. Then ₪29.90 monthly, ₪199.90 yearly, or ₪399.90 once for life. Everything included; your AI chat subscription is not. iPhone with iOS 17 or later; Apple Watch optional. The app is in Hebrew only and sold on the Israeli App Store. An English edition is planned.
